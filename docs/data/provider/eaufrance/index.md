## License

The [Terms and Conditions](https://hubeau.eaufrance.fr/page/conditions-generales) of Hubeau put the
re-use of its datasets under the [Etalab Open License](https://www.data.gouv.fr/pages/legal/licences/etalab-2-0)
and ask the user to cite the author of the data. Hub'Eau states no date of an update, so `data_updated_at` of the
`metadata` of its answers is `null`.

The same terms ask of a caller (read 2026-11-06, French; translated by us): the API runs on "a pooled technical
infrastructure", so the user "must take care to use the APIs to download a subset of the datasets", with the filters
offered (by place, time, parameter, fields) and compressed transfers; where the whole dataset is needed, the user is
invited to take the bulk export files of the national data banks. The publisher "reserves the possibility of refusing
access to the APIs in case of abusive use". The terms state no number of requests. They also say the datasets "contain
raw data, that is, provided without reprocessing", as produced by the contributors, who "alone are responsible" for it;
Wetterdienst reshapes them, which its `processing_note` says.
