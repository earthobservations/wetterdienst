The terms (read 2026-11-06, German; translated by us) say the information "may be taken over" on the basis of
DL-DE->Zero-2.0, that the data may be "reproduced, printed, presented, changed, processed and passed on to third parties"
for commercial and non-commercial use, and that "this is done on the basis of the licence"; they ask for no credit.
They also say the webservices provide "unchecked raw data" without liability or warranty for correctness or timeliness,
and state no limit on requests. Wetterdienst's `attribution` for this provider is empty for that reason; a reader who
publishes the data should still say where it is from, and that it is unchecked.
