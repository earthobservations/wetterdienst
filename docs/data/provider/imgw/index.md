## License

IMGW-PIB publishes its data under terms of its own, the "Regulamin udostępniania danych" printed on the
[open data portal](https://danepubliczne.imgw.pl/) (in Polish), on the legal basis of the Polish act of
11 August 2021 on open data and the re-use of public sector information. They are no standard licence, so the
response metadata of the REST API has `license.spdx_id: null` with the name and the link of the terms.

- **Credit**: every work made with the data carries „Źródłem pochodzenia danych jest Instytut Meteorologii i
  Gospodarki Wodnej – Państwowy Instytut Badawczy" (§ 5), and for data you changed also „Dane Instytutu
  Meteorologii i Gospodarki Wodnej – Państwowego Instytutu Badawczego zostały przetworzone".
- **Use**: the terms make the data free for private use, and for any purpose where it is "dane o wysokiej wartości"
  (high-value data, § 3 and § 5); other use for business needs an agreement with IMGW-PIB (§ 3).
  Whether the observations served here are high-value data is not stated; ask IMGW-PIB before relying on it for a
  commercial use. The Polish open data portal dane.gov.pl lists some of the datasets as CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and others as
  CC0 1.0, which the terms do not.
