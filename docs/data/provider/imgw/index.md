## License

IMGW-PIB names the Polish act of 11 August 2021 on open data and the re-use of public sector
information as the legal basis of publishing its data, under which "everyone has the right to re-use public
sector information" ([terms of the open data portal](https://dane.imgw.pl/), in Polish). It names no licence,
so the response metadata of the REST API has `license: null` for IMGW.
